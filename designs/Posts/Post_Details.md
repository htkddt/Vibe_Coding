# Post Details — UX Description

## User goal

Read a post, assess engagement, and take the appropriate management action.

## Core experience

- The main content card shows the post title, author, date, status, body, and media.
- Reactions and comments enable lightweight engagement.
- The performance panel displays views, reactions, comments, and trend data.
- Post actions include editing, sharing, or navigating to related management actions.

## Expected behavior

- Status is expressed in text as well as colour.
- Editing is available only to authorized users; unavailable actions explain why.
- Analytics use accessible labels/tooltips and safely handle no-data periods.

## Navigation

Opened from any Posts list, Dashboard feed, or a direct notification.
