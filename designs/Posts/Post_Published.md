# Posts — Published UX Description

## User goal

Review live content and understand which posts need attention.

## Core experience

- The Published tab lists live posts with author, publication time, and published status.
- Search and sort help users locate a specific announcement.
- Opening a row leads to Post details and its performance view.
- The overview panel shows published vs. draft totals and promotes the next content action.

## Expected behavior

- Published status remains legible in every state.
- Editing or unpublishing must be permission-aware and include confirmation for high-impact actions.
- No-published-posts state encourages creation without hiding the drafts workflow.

## Navigation

Part of Posts; routes to Post details and Create post.
