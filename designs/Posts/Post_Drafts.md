# Posts — Drafts UX Description

## User goal

Resume, organize, or discard unfinished posts safely.

## Core experience

- The Drafts tab lists unfinished posts with title, saved context, status, and last update.
- Selecting a draft opens the editor; the overflow menu offers quick secondary actions.
- The side overview highlights draft volume and provides a **Finish post** entry point.

## Expected behavior

- Autosave or explicit save status must be visible.
- Deleting a draft requires confirmation and offers recovery where feasible.
- Draft items sorted by last modified help the user resume the most relevant work.

## Navigation

Part of Posts; routes directly into Edit draft.
